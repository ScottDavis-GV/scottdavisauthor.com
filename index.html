About Scott

Author photo

Scott Davis served in the United States Navy aboard submarines, SSBN 611 and SSN 590, as a Quartermaster, the rating responsible for navigation, charts, and dead reckoning.

He used the GI Bill to earn a degree in Electronic Engineering, then a Master's in Computer Science. He worked forty years as an electronic and computer engineer. When he retired, he began teaching Computer Science at Sacramento State.

He has wanted to write novels since he was a teenager. It took him a while. Dead Reckoning is his first novel.

He lives in Nevada County, California, in the Sierra foothills. His books are published by Declination Press.
