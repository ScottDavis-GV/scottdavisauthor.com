Privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics, advertising, or third-party fonts or scripts. It doesn't collect anything about you. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers, like any web server, see your IP address in order to send you the page; see GitHub's privacy statement.

The newsletter

If you join the newsletter, I keep your email address so I can send it to you, and I give it to the service that sends my email for that purpose only. I don't sell it or share it with anyone else. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes your address.

When you email me

If you write to me, I keep your message and address to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. Writing to me doesn't add you to any list.
