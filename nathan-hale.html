The Nathan Hale series

A shipwright who reads damage for a living.

Nathan Hale was a Naval Intelligence officer. Now he lives aboard a wooden cutter in Port Townsend, Washington, repairs boats, and keeps a nightly radio check-in with a handful of veterans who need to say they are still alive. The series follows what happens when the pattern-reading he walked away from keeps finding him. No gadgets. Paper charts, cold water, and the cost of knowing when the official story is a lie.

The world

Port Townsend and the Salish Sea

The books are set in and around the working waterfront of Port Townsend, the Strait of Juan de Fuca, Admiralty Inlet, and the San Juan Islands, with the weather, tides, and small-craft realities of those waters kept honest. Real places appear and are used fictitiously. Nathan's boat, Sculpin, is a heavy-displacement double-ender in the Colin Archer tradition, which matters more than once.
